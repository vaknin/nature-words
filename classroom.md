# What to post in Google Classroom

**One post, one link, for the whole class.** Every kid opens the same link, takes a short quiz, and the site
tells him where to start ("Start at River! 🌊 Well done!"). Nothing is posted for you: the class teacher (or you)
pastes this once, about 10 minutes before the lesson.

The link: **https://vaknin.github.io/nature-words/**

## Click by click
1. Open the class, then the **Classwork** tab.
2. Press **+ Create**, then **Assignment** (an assignment gives each kid a "Mark as done" button, your live list of who finished).
3. **Title:** `🌲 Nature Words`
4. **Instructions:** paste:
   ```
   🎧 Headphones on.
   1. Press the link.
   2. Press Start.
   3. Play!
   4. At the end, come back here and press "Mark as done".

   שימו אוזניות, לחצו על הקישור ואז על Start. בסוף חזרו לכאן ולחצו על "סימון כבוצע".
   ```
   The Hebrew name of the button is from memory: copy it from your own Classroom screen.
5. Under **Attach**, press **Link**, paste `https://vaknin.github.io/nature-words/`, press **Add link**. Keep it the
   only attachment, so the kid has exactly one thing to press.
6. **Points:** Ungraded. **Due:** no due date. **For:** all students of this class.
7. Press **Assign** (or the arrow next to it, then **Schedule**, for 10 minutes before class: scheduled posts can
   arrive a few minutes late).

## The three places, for you only
The site places each kid by itself. To move a kid by hand, add the ending to the address on his laptop:
- River: https://vaknin.github.io/nature-words/#level-1
- Forest: https://vaknin.github.io/nature-words/#level-2
- Mountain: https://vaknin.github.io/nature-words/#level-3

After 5 right first tries in a row a kid is offered the next place up, and after 3 questions in a row with a
miss the place below; each offer comes once and he chooses.

## In class
1. On your laptop open `https://vaknin.github.io/nature-words/#teacher`, press **F** for full screen on the
   projector, and press **2** then **Space** to test the sound.
2. At "Quiz time", the kids open **Classwork → 🌲 Nature Words → the link → Start**.

## If something goes wrong
- **No sound on a laptop:** the voices are recorded clips inside the page, so it is the laptop's volume or
  headphones, not the internet.
- **The internet drops:** a page already open keeps working; it needs no connection once loaded.
- **A kid closed the tab:** he opens the link again and presses **Continue**: the laptop remembers where he was today.
- **The microphone is blocked:** the "say it" game hides the record button and keeps **Next**.
