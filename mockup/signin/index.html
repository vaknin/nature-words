Your words, the brief for this page

Nothing is picked yet: you choose

  • Three looks below, each with its own colours, letters, icon set and two logo ideas. Mixing is fine: say "B's colours with C's animals".
  • Every look shows the same class-only flow: the code on the projector, tap your name, make your secret buddy, you're in.
  • Try the two live parts in each look: tap a name and watch it pop out of the list; then make a secret buddy.
  • Secret buddy = an animal plus one of 6 colours: 96 choices instead of 16, so a guesser's chance in 6 tries drops from 37.5% to 6.25%, for one extra tap.
  • No emoji anywhere: every picture is a glyph from an open-source icon set, credited at the bottom.
