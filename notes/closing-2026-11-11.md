# What changed on the nature lesson since tonight's first build

Live link (checked live just now): https://vaknin.github.io/nature-words/
Every screen below was looked at one by one; the screenshots are in design/screens in the lesson's repo.

- **A real design pass and new fonts:** Fredoka for words and buttons, Rubik for Hebrew and running text, both built into the page so they show with no internet. Proof: design/screens/01-start-new.png, and the live link (both fonts load there).
- **Clean header:** one row with the place, the path to the flag and the points. "LV1", "x1" and the extra trophy text are gone. Proof: design/screens/22-flag-in-header.png
- **Full screen button with a hint** on the start page, telling kids to press it. Proof: design/screens/01-start-new.png, and on the live link.
- **Never record:** no Record, no Hear me, no microphone. The say game plays the word and shows "Say it out loud!", then Next. Proof: design/screens/08-river-1-say.png
- **Speaker shows on the listen tile:** the empty white square is now a big round speaker in the place's colour. Proof: design/screens/17-mountain-2-listen.png
- **Pictures tied to their words:** a word pill under each sentence ("dry = יבש") shows which word the picture is. Proof: design/screens/16-mountain-1-say.png
- **Touch first:** every button big enough for a finger and spaced apart, letters can be tapped or dragged, and each word's speaker is its own button. Proof: design/screens/10-river-3-picword.png and design/screens/11-river-4-build.png
- **Race boards online, one top 5 per place:** shared by the whole class across laptops, a new top score asks for a first name, and without internet a laptop shows its own board. Proof: design/screens/29-race-board-new-row.png and design/screens/31-race-end-offline.png
- **Reset for the race boards:** a button on your projector view clears all three boards for the class. Proof: the live link with #teacher at the end (checked: the button is there).
- **Back button fixed:** it now sits inside the race card instead of spilling out. Proof: design/screens/29-race-board-new-row.png
- **No teacher link where kids look:** the kids' start page has no Teacher button; your projector view is only on your own link. Proof: design/screens/01-start-new.png
- **Classroom guide for the class teacher, Word and PDF:** one post, one link, click by click, and it tells kids to press Full screen. Proof: Nature-Words-Classroom-Guide.docx and Nature-Words-Classroom-Guide.pdf in your laptop's Downloads.
- **Your lesson-plan form, filled, without microphone lines:** Proof: LP.docx and LP.pdf in your laptop's Downloads.
- **The teacher's plan matches the site's look and no longer says record.** Proof: design/screens/44-plan-top.png and design/screens/45-plan-stage-4.png
- **New English voices made through the better voice chain:** all 22 clips, boy and girl, each checked by a listening model word for word. They are made but not in the page yet: the live lesson still plays the earlier voices, and putting the new ones in is the next step.
