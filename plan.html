Nature Words: teacher's plan

English, 5th grade · 40-45 minutes · every kid on his own laptop through Google Classroom · site: vaknin.github.io/nature-words · projector: #teacher

Lesson header

TopicNature words: air, dry, forest, grass, mountain, nature, river, sea
Main objectiveBy the end of the lesson, pupils can say, read and spell eight nature words (air, dry, forest, grass, mountain, nature, river, sea) and use them in one short spoken sentence.
Global Can-Do (Pre-A1 to A1)Can recognise familiar words with pictures; can understand short instructions given slowly with gestures; can produce short phrases on a familiar topic. (Wording from memory: match it to the Ministry curriculum before sending.)
Operative Can-DoI can… 1. say the eight words from a picture or gesture; 2. find the picture or word when I hear or read it; 3. build each word from letters; 4. say "Let's go to the sea!" about a place I choose.
LevelsThree places by readiness, set by an 8-question quiz on the site: River (#level-1) the most support, Forest (#level-2), Mountain (#level-3) the most stretch. Never called easy or hard. Offers to move after 5 first tries in a row (up) or 3 questions with a miss in a row (down); the kid chooses.
MaterialsProjector + teacher's laptop (site, #teacher); a laptop and headphones per pupil; the Classroom post (classroom.md); board and marker; this plan.
InteractionWhole class (T-Ss, choral, stand-up groups), then alone on the laptop, then whole class.

Before class (5 minutes)

  1. The class teacher posts the Assignment 10 minutes before (classroom.md has the note to send her).
  2. Open #teacher on the projector, F for full screen, 2 then Space to test the sound, then 1.
  3. Laptops closed, headphones on the desk until stage 3.

Keys: 1-6 stage · → next item / next stage · ← back · Space show and say / hide and show / start the timer · F full screen.

The lesson, stage by stage (4 + 8 + 5 + 18 + 4 + 4 = 43 minutes)

1 · Welcome to nature · 4 min · whole class · opening

Projector: title, goal and agenda.

"Good morning! Laptops closed. Eyes on me." · "Look. Trees, water, a big sky. This is nature." (open arms) · "Who likes a trip to the sea? Hands up! To the mountains?" · "You know nature words in English. Tell me!" (write six on the board; the class repeats each) · "Today: eight NEW nature words. Read with me." (the class reads the goal) · "First we meet the words. Then a quiz and a game on the laptop. Then we talk." · "Who can say in Hebrew what we do today?"

Every kid: hands up, calls out, repeats, reads. If silent: draw a sun and a tree: "What is this?"

Transition: "Stand up!" Praise: "You know so many nature words already!"

2 · Meet the words · 8 min · whole class, choral, standing · repetition

Projector: one word card at a time, in this order: nature, mountain, river, forest, sea, grass, air, dry (look-alike meanings apart). Then a ninth slide with all eight pictures, no words.

Per word, about 35 seconds: 1. "Look. Listen." Space: the child voice says it and the word appears. 2. Say it twice with the gesture. 3. "Everybody!" three times: normal, whisper, loud. 4. "What is it in Hebrew?" The class answers.

Gestures: nature, open your arms wide · mountain, hands meet in a peak · river, hand swims like a snake · forest, arms up like trees · sea, big waves with both arms · grass, wiggle your fingers low · air, blow on your hand · dry, wipe your forehead.

On the ninth slide: Show me! "I say the word. You show me with your hands. No talking!" Get faster. Say it! "I show, you say." End with a trick: point at the mountain and ask "Sea?" The class: "No! Mountain!"

What the rest of the class does: everyone says and gestures every word, at least four times each.

Transition: "Sit down. Don't open the laptop yet. Look at the screen." Praise: "Beautiful gestures, and I heard everyone's voice!"

3 · Quiz time · 5 min · demo, then alone · recognition

Projector: the three places; then, in a second tab, demo one quiz question and one "Listen. Say the word." on the kid page.

"Look. Three places: River, Forest and Mountain. All three have the same eight words. First, a quick quiz. Then the laptop gives you a place to start. It is a good start for YOU today. Your place can change later, and that is OK. Points and cards are the same in every place. Eyes on your own screen."

Demo: "I listen. I press. No right or wrong in the quiz, just try. When you see the microphone, press Allow."

On four fingers: "One: open your laptop. Two: Classroom, 'Nature Words'. Three: press the link. Four: headphones on, press Start." · "Alone or with a friend?" (Alone.) "Who can say the four steps in Hebrew?" Then "Go!"

Praise: "Quick and quiet. Thank you!"

4 · Play and race · 18 min · alone on laptops; teacher walks around · repetition → recognition → reproduction

Projector: the eight pictures with words and an 18-minute bar moving left to right (Space starts it).

GameRiver #level-1Forest #level-2Mountain #level-3
1. Listen. Say the word. (repetition)picture, word, Hebrew word; record 3 s; "Hear me"samepicture and sentence; record 6 s
2. Listen. Press… (recognition)3 pictures6 pictures6 written words
3. Look / Read. Press the word. (recognition)picture → 3 words, a speaker on eachpicture → 4 wordssentence gap → 4 words
4. Build the word. (reproduction)first letter given, short words firstall letters2 extra letters, no speaker
🏁 Last check4 questions, first try counts; then "Go to Classroom. Press 'Mark as done'."

Eight rounds per game; a missed word comes back once at the end of its game. Points: +5 per word said, +10 for a right first try (×2 from 3 in a row, ×3 from 5), +5 right after a miss; nothing is ever taken away. A card per word gets three stars (said, found, built) and turns gold. After the Last check: a 30-second race (picture → 1 of 4 words, the same for every place, one press per question) against "Your best" and the teacher's score, or the next place.

At 11 minutes: "Look, a flag! Finish your word. Press the flag: four last questions." · At 13 minutes: "After the flag you can race: 30 seconds. Beat your best! Or go to the next place. You choose."

Teacher, while they work: first two minutes, every screen on the quiz or a game, sound on, microphone allowed. Then River screens first: "Play me your word." One specific praise for effort per kid. At an offer: "Good choice" either way. In the race minutes, praise "New best!". Note three names for "Why?" in stage 5.

Transition at 18: "Stop. Headphones off. Screens half down. Eyes on me." Praise: "You tried again and you got it. That is how we learn."

5 · Let's go! · 4 min · whole class, stand-up groups · reproduction in a sentence

Projector: "Let's go to the ___!" with two pictures; three slides: sea or mountain; river or forest; all four.

"The sea or the mountain? Show me with your hands!" · "Say it, everybody: Let's go to the…!" · "Sea people, stand up! Tell them!" · To one or two volunteers: "Why the sea?" Say the answer back in full; the class repeats. · Last slide: "Four places, one class trip. On three, say your sentence, loud!"

Every kid: gestures, says about six sentences, stands with his group. Praise: "Full sentences from everyone. That is real English!"

6 · What's missing? · 4 min · whole class · reproduction, sum-up

Projector: the eight pictures; Space hides one, Space again brings it back with its word and voice. Four rounds, then → for the summary.

"Who beat their own best in the race? Stand up!" (applause) · "Close your eyes! … Open! What's missing?" ×4 · "How many new words today? Eight! Say all eight with your hands, fast!" · "Read the goal. Thumbs up, middle or down. How many stars in your last check? Show me with your fingers." · "Open your screen. Press 'Mark as done'. Close your laptop."

Praise: "Eight new words in one lesson. You worked hard in every place. I am proud of you!"

Covering the evaluation form

Covered: objective and Can-Dos; prior knowledge (stage 1); presentation, practice, production (2, 3-4, 5); relevance (class trips); topic, goal and agenda on screen; sequenced instructions, repeated in Hebrew by a pupil; three levels; a praise line per stage; online interactive tools; a closing that sums up; the Last check as proof of the objective.

Weak, by tonight's decisions: no pair work; "discussion" is only "Why?" to a few volunteers. New risk: a race in an observed lesson; it is opt-in, against your own best, and praised for new bests, not rank.

Cannot cover: the deadlines (a week to the advisor, two days to the COT), your voice, posture and discipline on the day, and the self-reflection (within 2 days after).

Reflection points: how many kids in each place, how many moved, Last check stars, how many spoke in stage 5.

If something fails

Dead laptop: that kid copies and draws the eight words from the projector. No network: run stages 2, 5 and 6 longer. Minutes left: "Simon says: river!" with the gestures.
