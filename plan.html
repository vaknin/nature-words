Nature Trails: teacher's plan

English, 5th grade · 40-45 minutes · every kid on his own laptop through Google Classroom · site: vaknin.github.io/nature-words · projector: #teacher

Lesson header

TopicNature words: air, dry, forest, grass, mountain, nature, river, sea
Main objectiveBy the end of the lesson pupils will be able to recognise, say and spell eight nature words (air, dry, forest, grass, mountain, nature, river, sea) and match each to its picture.
Global Can-DoPupils can understand and use basic words about the world around them (Israeli English curriculum, Pre-A1/A1, reception and production).
Operative Can-DoI can say the eight nature words when I see their pictures. · I can pick the right picture when I hear a word. · I can spell the words with letter tiles.
LevelsThree trails by readiness, decided by a one-minute start quiz on the site: River (#level-1) more support, Forest (#level-2), Mountain (#level-3) the most challenge. Never called easy or hard.
MaterialsProjector + teacher's laptop (site, #teacher); a laptop and headphones per pupil; the Google Classroom post (classroom.md); this plan.
InteractionWhole class (T-Ss, choral), then individual work on laptops (S-computer), then whole class.

Before class (5 minutes)

  1. Post the Classroom material (one post, one link, see classroom.md) 10 minutes before.
  2. Open #teacher on the projector, press F for full screen, press 2 then Space to test the sound, then 1.
  3. Laptops closed and headphones on the desk until stage 3.

Keys: 1-5 stage · → next word / next stage · ← back · Space show and say the word · F full screen.

The lesson, stage by stage

1 · Welcome to nature · 4 min · whole class

Aim: context and prior knowledge; pupils see the topic, goal and agenda.

"Good morning! Close your eyes. You are outside. What do you see? Trees? Water? Tell me in English or show me!"

Take 3-4 answers; write the English ones they already know. Show the goal and agenda on the screen and read the goal together.

Feedback: "Great, you already know nature words!" Transition: "Let's meet eight new nature words."

2 · Meet the words · 10 min · whole class, choral

Aim: presentation and repetition: hear, see and say every word.

"Look at the picture. What is it? ... Listen. (Space) Mountain. Say it with me: mountain! Do it with me!" (gesture)

Per word: picture first (pupils guess), Space shows and says it, the class repeats twice with the gesture (blow on your hand, wipe your forehead, arms up like trees, wiggle fingers low, hands meet in a peak, open arms wide, hand swims like a snake, big waves). Then →.

Last minute: "I do the gesture, you say the word" for all eight, fast.

What the rest of the class does: everyone says and gestures every word; no kid only watches.

Feedback: "Wow, loud and clear!" Transition: "Now each of you goes on a nature trail. Open your laptops."

3 · Find your trail · 4 min · individual

Aim: place every pupil by readiness, without labels.

"Open Classroom. Press Nature Trails, then the link, then Start. Headphones on! Answer the quiz and find your trail."

אם צריך: "פתחו את קלאסרום, לחצו על Nature Trails, על הקישור ואז Start. שימו אוזניות."

Demonstrate once on the projector (press Start, answer one question). The quiz is six quick questions; it ends with "Your trail is River / Forest / Mountain! Well done!" Walk around and help with logging in.

Feedback: "Every trail is a real trail, let's go!" Transition: pupils press Start on their trail.

4 · On the trails · 20 min · individual, on laptops

Aim: practice (recognition) and production (spelling), at each pupil's level.

TrailPicture → wordListen → pickBuild the word
River #level-13 words, each with a speaker to hear it3 picturesfirst letter given, speaker on
Forest #level-24 words6 picturesall letters, speaker on
Mountain #level-3all 8 words6 written words (no pictures)all letters + 2 extra letters, no sound

Eight rounds per game, one per word. Six right first tries in a row offer the next trail up; three misses in a row offer another trail; the pupil chooses. Every trail ends with "Finished? Try the next trail!"

Teacher: circulate; praise effort ("You built 'mountain' by yourself!"); ask pupils to say each word they hear quietly.

Transition: two minutes before the end: "Two more minutes! Finish your game."

5 · Nature words show · 5 min · whole class, speaking

Aim: closure and production: every pupil says the words.

"Laptops down. I point, you say the word and do the gesture! ... Now: what is your favourite place in nature? 'I like the sea.'"

All eight pictures on screen: point in random order, the class says and gestures. Then 3-4 pupils say "I like the ___." Read the goal again: "Can you say, read and spell eight nature words? Thumbs up!"

Feedback: praise by trail effort, never by trail name.

Covering the evaluation form

Objective and Can-Dos in the header; opening activates prior knowledge (stage 1); presentation, practice, production (stages 2, 4, 5); theme from their lives (nature they visit); speaking in stages 1, 2 and 5; three levels plus a move up or down (stage 4); varied interaction (whole class, choral, individual); topic, goal and agenda on screen. Not covered by any plan: your voice, posture and handling of discipline in the room.

Deadlines: plan to the pedagogical advisor a week before, to the COT two days before, self-reflection within 2 days after.

Reflection points: did the start quiz place pupils well (how many moved trails)? Which words were hardest to build? Did the weaker pupils use the speaker help?

One tip

Before class, open each trail link once on a pupil laptop with headphones, so you have seen what they see.
